# B9.7 Known Limits

## Current limits

- the expanded consumer model remains internal-only; there is no public ingress
  or edge surface here
- the research consumer is intentionally narrower than the operator path
- only approved profile identifiers are supported; raw scheduler payloads remain
  out of scope
- the bridge surface remains operator-only in this phase
- this phase proves bounded consumer expansion, not broad self-service

## Interpretation

B9.7 is about proving that the Beagle/Darwin stack can serve more than one
controlled consumer path without relaxing the already-closed backplane
boundaries.
