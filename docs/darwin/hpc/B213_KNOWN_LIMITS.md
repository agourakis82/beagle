# B21.3 Known Limits

- The Crossref export is a compatible stub, not a deposit payload proven against live Crossref infrastructure.
- The DataCite export is ready-shaped metadata, not a DOI registration workflow.
- The RO-Crate export is bounded and campaign-scoped; it does not broaden into a general release platform.
- `claim-linked-human-eval-pending` remains visible when it still applies and is intentionally treated as a warning, not hidden.
